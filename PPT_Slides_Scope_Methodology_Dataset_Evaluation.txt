SLIDE — METHODOLOGY
================================================================================
HEADLINE: Methodology

SLIDE TEXT:
• Phase 1 — Dataset: Design and generate printed character dataset for Hindi and Telugu with two fonts per script, multiple sizes, and controlled variations.
• Phase 2 — Preprocessing: Resize to fixed resolution (128×128), normalize, optional grayscale; maintain train/validation split.
• Phase 3 — Model: Implement convolutional encoder-decoder; VAE with reparameterization, KL divergence and reconstruction loss; tune latent dimension and architecture.
• Phase 4 — Evaluation: Reconstruction error (MSE/BCE); visual quality of reconstructions and generated samples; latent space visualization (e.g. t-SNE) and interpolation.
• Phase 5 — Analysis: Compare AE vs VAE; script-wise and font-wise analysis; document limitations and future work.

SLIDE — DATASET COLLECTION (DESIGN)
================================================================================
HEADLINE: Dataset Design

SLIDE TEXT:
• Per script: 13 alphabets × 2 fonts × 4 font sizes × 20 samples = 2,080 images.
• Font sizes: 10 pt, 14 pt, 18 pt, 22 pt (covers small to large print).
• Variations (per size): One clean base sample; remaining 19 with one or more of — rotation (±3°), translation (±3 px), scaling (94–106%), stroke width (dilate/erode), contrast (95–105%), gamma (95–105%).
• Uniqueness: Hash-based duplicate detection so each of the 20 samples per (alphabet, font, size) is distinct.
• Rendering: High DPI (300), tight crop, resize to 128×128 with character filling ~99.7% of canvas for consistent, character-centric images.
