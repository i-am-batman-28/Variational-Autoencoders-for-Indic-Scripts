DATASET SPECIFICATIONS
================================================================================

1. SAMPLE COUNT PER ALPHABET
---------------------------
Recommended: 50-100 samples per alphabet per font

Breakdown:
  - Base samples: 30 (as per your requirement)
  - With variations: 50-100 per alphabet
  - Total per font: 600-1200 images (12 alphabets × 50-100)
  - Total for both fonts: 1200-2400 images

Why 50-100?
  ✅ Enough variation for VAE to learn distribution
  ✅ Good train/val/test split (70/15/15)
  ✅ Robust to outliers
  ✅ Allows for diverse augmentations
  ✅ Production-grade quality

Minimum viable: 30 per alphabet (360 total per font)
Recommended: 50 per alphabet (600 total per font)
Optimal: 75-100 per alphabet (900-1200 total per font)

================================================================================
2. VARIATIONS & AUGMENTATIONS
================================================================================

For each base character, generate variations:

A. GEOMETRIC TRANSFORMATIONS (Essential)
   -------------------------------------
   1. Rotation: ±2 to ±5 degrees (small, realistic)
      - Why: Simulates slight printing variations
      - Count: 3-5 variations (no rotation, slight left, slight right)
   
   2. Translation: ±2-3 pixels (horizontal/vertical)
      - Why: Simulates positioning variations
      - Count: 5 variations (center, 4 directions)
   
   3. Slight Scaling: 95% to 105% (very small)
      - Why: Simulates font size variations
      - Count: 3 variations (95%, 100%, 105%)

B. NOISE & QUALITY VARIATIONS (Important)
   ---------------------------------------
   1. Gaussian Noise: Very light (σ=0.01-0.02)
      - Why: Simulates scanning/printing noise
      - Count: 2-3 variations
   
   2. Blur: Very slight (kernel size 1-2)
      - Why: Simulates different print quality
      - Count: 2 variations (none, slight blur)
   
   3. Contrast/Brightness: ±5% adjustment
      - Why: Simulates different printing conditions
      - Count: 3 variations (normal, darker, lighter)

C. RENDERING VARIATIONS (Production Quality)
   ------------------------------------------
   1. Anti-aliasing: Different levels
      - Why: Different rendering engines
      - Count: 2 variations (standard, enhanced)
   
   2. DPI variations: 72, 96, 150 DPI
      - Why: Different output resolutions
      - Count: 3 variations
   
   3. Background variations: Pure white, off-white
      - Why: Different paper/scanning conditions
      - Count: 2 variations

D. COMBINATIONS (For Rich Dataset)
   --------------------------------
   - Combine 2-3 variations together
   - Example: Rotation + slight noise + contrast adjustment
   - Creates more diverse samples

VARIATION STRATEGY:
  Base: 1 clean sample
  Single variations: ~15-20 variations
  Combinations: ~10-15 variations
  Total: ~30-40 variations per base character

To get 50-100 samples per alphabet:
  - Start with 2-3 base renderings (different DPI/anti-aliasing)
  - Apply variations to each
  - Result: 50-100 diverse samples

================================================================================
3. FONT SIZES
================================================================================

Primary Size: 12pt (as per your requirement)
Additional Sizes: 10pt, 14pt (for robustness)

Why multiple sizes?
  ✅ Makes model more robust
  ✅ Better generalization
  ✅ Handles different input sizes
  ✅ Production-grade quality

Distribution:
  - 12pt: 60% of samples (primary requirement)
  - 10pt: 20% of samples (smaller variation)
  - 14pt: 20% of samples (larger variation)

For 50 samples per alphabet:
  - 12pt: 30 samples
  - 10pt: 10 samples
  - 14pt: 10 samples

================================================================================
4. IMAGE DIMENSIONS & RESOLUTION
================================================================================

Output Size: 128x128 pixels (as you've been using)

Why 128x128?
  ✅ Good balance of detail and training speed
  ✅ Standard for character recognition
  ✅ Sufficient for VAE learning
  ✅ Production-grade resolution

Rendering Resolution:
  - Render at higher DPI (150-300 DPI)
  - Then resize to 128x128
  - Preserves quality better than rendering directly at 128x128

Process:
  1. Render character at high resolution (e.g., 512x512 at 300 DPI)
  2. Apply variations
  3. Resize to 128x128 using Lanczos
  4. Final output: 128x128 PNG
